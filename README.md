FitAI — an iOS app that generates personalized workout and diet plans in seconds based on your goals, experience level, available equipment, and workout duration.


I used to waste time jumping between websites and blogs just to figure out a workout and diet plan that matched my fitness goals.

So, I built my own solution.

**FitAI** — an iOS app that generates personalized workout and diet plans in seconds based on your goals, experience level, available equipment, and workout duration.

Built with **SwiftUI**, **OpenAI API**, **MVVM**, **Async/Await**, and **Core Data**.

It was a fun project that let me explore AI integration while building something I genuinely use myself. 😊
